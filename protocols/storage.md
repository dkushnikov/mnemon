# Storage Protocol

Superseded by the vault store design: [`docs/specs/2026-10-16-vault-store.md`](../docs/specs/2026-10-16-vault-store.md) (S1 — entities and ownership) and the capture contracts: [`docs/specs/2026-10-15-capture-contracts.md`](../docs/specs/2026-10-15-capture-contracts.md) (§3.1 identity, §3.3 publication).

What holds:

- **One revision per folder:** `Sources/<YYYY-MM-DD>_<hash8>[-n]/` with `source.md` (immutable: its body is the captured text byte for byte, `capture.text_sha256`) and `extract.md` (the engine's validated output; a person edits only the curation fields — `status`, `index_note`, `title`, `tags`, `domains`, `people`, `visibility`, `rating`). `hash8` is a folder label; the identity is `resource_sha256` (`sha256("<kind>:" + key)`: a URL's canonical form v1, a text's hash, a file's hash). A re-capture is a new folder naming its predecessor in `supersedes:`; folders are never renamed, moved or deleted.
- **The engine is the only writer** of `Sources/`, `index.md` (regenerated by `mnemon reindex`; replaced rows are kept in `_inputs/index-hand-edits-<date>.md`), the archive (`archive_dir/<revision>.<ext>`, `<revision>.report.json`, `<revision>.site/`) and the receipts (`~/.local/state/mnemon/vaults/<id>/captures/`). `Synthesis/`, `_inputs/`, `_meta/`, `_claude/` and Bases belong to the vault's people and agents.
- **Legacy folders** (published before the capture contracts) are matched by the URL in `url:` and never rewritten; a managed revision of such a resource is a new folder.
- `_inputs/pending-writes/` is no longer a queue the engine drains; `mnemon doctor --data` counts its entries as legacy (`human/queues`).

`mnemon doctor --data` checks these invariants and prints what it finds; it never repairs.
